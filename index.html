Behind the numbers

Our statistics & formulas

Explore the definitions behind our statistics. Each metric measures a different part of team performance.

Points per turnover PPT

PPT = average of [game points ÷ max(game turnovers, 1)]

Each game receives a points-per-turnover score. Those scores are averaged with equal weight across the team’s rated games. Higher PPT ranks higher. A zero-turnover game uses a denominator of one.

PPT measures scoring relative to turnovers. It does not adjust for opponent strength or pace. PPT is the average of individual game ratios, rather than the ratio of season averages.

Game coverage: “Rated / played” shows how many games have usable points and turnover data, compared with the reported season total. Missing box scores are excluded. Division I membership uses a provisional conference filter.
